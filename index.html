1. Introduction

A tree is a non-linear hierarchy consisting of nodes. The topmost node is called the Root, and every node (except the root) is connected by a directed edge from exactly one other node.

Root The starting node.
Leaf Node with no children.
Parent/Child Relational hierarchy.
